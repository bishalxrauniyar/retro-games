Good to know

How it works

  • No account, no ads

    Every game is a single page that runs entirely in your browser. We never ask for your email.

  • Keyboard and touch

    Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to act. On a phone, use the on-screen pad or swipe.

  • Scores stay local

    Your high scores are kept in this browser only, so nothing is uploaded and nothing can leak.
